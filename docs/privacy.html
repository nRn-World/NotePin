Privacy Policy – NotePin

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Developer: nRn World (bynrnworld@gmail.com)

Summary

NotePin does not collect, store, or transmit your notes to our servers. Notes stay on your device (and optionally in your personal browser sync profile).

Data stored on your device

  • Sticky notes (text, position, color, page URL / anchor info)
  • Settings (language, storage scope, UI preferences)

Data is saved with the browser storage API (storage.local and optionally storage.sync for your personal sync account).

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No accounts on our servers
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties
  • No upload of your notes to NotePin / nRn World servers

Permissions

  • storage — save notes and settings on your device / browser sync
  • activeTab / content scripts — show and anchor notes on pages you use

Your control

  • Export or import notes as JSON
  • Delete notes or clear all NotePin data from settings
